## One-shot measurements and fixed comparisons

Run H.265 only, `medium` preset, 16 frames, stride 2, 128×128 source, five QPs `30,35,40,45,50`, and the original six candidates `identity128`, `area112`, `area96`, `area112_up128`, `blur020_128`, `blur040_128`. On every source and QP, encode/decode all six candidates and score `r2plus1d_18` and `r3d_18`. Select V2-C and V6 streams from their label-free observations using the frozen files. Correctness/labels must not enter either selector. Then encode/decode the identity, V2-C-selected and V6-selected streams for `mc3_18`; re-encoded bpp must match the primary cache. If two arms choose the same stream, infer it once and record reuse. No `mc3_18` prediction enters stream selection.

Report all three analyzers for the prespecified comparisons **V2-C vs identity**, **V6 vs identity**, and **V6 vs V2-C**. For primaries also report fixed `area96` and `area112` vs identity. Merge two 500-source shards into full 1,000-source curves **before** calculating BD-rate or intervals. Bootstrap 2,000 times with seed `20261001`, unit **source video**, retaining all QPs and arms paired in every draw. Report point BD-rate Top-1, BD-accuracy, minimum same-QP Top-1 gap, percentile 95% CI, valid draw counts, source/index/config/raw/result SHA-256 and Git commits. Do not edit `src/metrics/bd_rate.py` or its bootstrap method to improve results.

**Original gate unchanged:** at least one codec must have point BD-rate Top-1 **strictly below −15%** and BD-accuracy >0 on both primary analyzers against identity. This V6 study measures only H.265; report whether *this codec* passes, and do not claim the overall gate passes if it does not. The `mc3_18` direct V6 vs V2-C BD-rate and CI are the prespecified transfer readout. A negative point estimate with upper 95% CI <0 is strong evidence of transfer; other outcomes are inconclusive or negative as reported. The exploratory same-QP guard is ≥−1 percentage point. None of these secondary criteria changes the original gate. Every result, including failure, is final for this locked sample; further tuning requires a different holdout.
