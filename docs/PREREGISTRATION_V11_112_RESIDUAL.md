## Label-free proxy and policy

Apply the analyzer's exact image preparation to source and decoded clips: convert RGB uint8 to float in [0,1], bilinear resize to 112×112 with `align_corners=False`, then normalize channelwise by mean `(0.43216, 0.394666, 0.37645)` and standard deviation `(0.22803, 0.22145, 0.216989)`. Use all 16 frames. Let horizontal and vertical forward differences be Gx and Gy, and the interior four-neighbor Laplacian be L. For source tensors X and decoded tensors Z, compute Eg = (mean[(GxX−GxZ)²]+mean[(GyX−GyZ)²])/(mean[GxX²]+mean[GyX²]+mean[GxZ²]+mean[GyZ²]+1e−12), El = mean[(LX−LZ)²]/(mean[LX²]+mean[LZ²]+1e−12), and D112=(Eg+El)/2. Finite D112 lies in [0,2]. No label, classifier, classifier weight or `mc3_18` output is used to compute it. Record decoded SHA-256, bpp, coded bytes and time per trial.

Start from the frozen V6 stream v. At QPs in the allowed set only, switch to frozen V2-C stream b if b≠v, `D112(v)−D112(b)>tau`, `R(b)≤(1+slack)R(v)`, and `R(b)≤R(identity)`. Otherwise retain v. Strict comparisons and ties retain v. This policy reads only QP, measured rate, D112 and the two frozen choices. It cannot choose an unmeasured stream.

Exactly 36 configurations: `tau ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.025, 0.05}`, `slack ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05}`, and allowed QPs in `{30,35}`, `{30,35,40}`, or `{30,35,40,45,50}`. The Cartesian product and tuple order above are the complete search. No extra threshold, hand-picked video exception, feature model or post-hoc grid expansion is permitted.
